CSS Test 19: Float and Clear

1. float: left (text wraps right)

Expected: The red box floats to the left side. Text wraps around the right side of the box. The box is removed from normal flow and text fills the remaining space beside it.

float: left

This text wraps around the floated element on its right side. When an element is floated left, it moves to the left edge of its containing block, and inline content flows around it on the right. The text continues to wrap until it clears past the bottom of the floated element, at which point it fills the full width again. This is one of the oldest CSS layout techniques, originally designed for text-around-image layouts in web pages.

2. float: right (text wraps left)

Expected: The teal box floats to the right side. Text wraps around the left side of the box. The box is pushed to the right edge of the container.

float: right

This text wraps around the floated element on its left side. When an element is floated right, it moves to the right edge of its containing block, and inline content flows around it on the left. The text fills the space to the left of the float, and once past the bottom of the floated element, the text resumes filling the full container width. Float right is commonly used for pull quotes and sidebars.
